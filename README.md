# YYZ AVOP DA Practice

A static English-language practice app for the DA test. The official test format has 30 questions, including a map question. This app practices the written portion with a randomized bank of 87 airside-rules questions and six illustrated diagram questions; prepare for the map question separately with the official GTAA site map link.

## Practice format

- Mock Exam: 26 randomized rules questions and three randomized diagram questions (29 written questions).
- The question bank contains 87 rules questions and six diagram questions. Questions from the previous attempt on that device are skipped when enough fresh questions remain.
- Questions 27–29 are always three different diagram questions that share the same illustration for that attempt. The illustration group alternates between apron markings and right-of-way diagrams on consecutive attempts.
- Every question displays five answer choices in randomized order, including occasional valid “All of the above” answers and “None of the above” distractors.
- Rule question order and answer choices are randomized each session.
- The overall exam format allows up to three errors across 30 questions, and all map items must be correct. The map question is not scored by this app.
- Missed questions show the selected answer, correct answer, and explanation after completion.
- Results include a prominent **Review All Answers** button that opens every question, the selected and correct answers, and its explanation.
- Progress and scores are not saved.

## Home-screen installation

The app includes a web manifest, Android app icons, an Apple touch icon, and the `AVOP DA` short name for Add to Home Screen. The home-screen icon features enlarged `AVOP` and `DA` lettering.

## Difficulty and source basis

Questions were independently written from the supplied AVOP DA practice notes and checked against the supplied Airport Traffic Directives – AVOP DA (2026). Diagram questions use the user-provided apron-marking and right-of-way illustrations. The home screen links to the official April 2024 GTAA site map for map study.

This is an unofficial personal study aid, not an exam replica or a GTAA-approved product. Follow the latest official directives and airside signs.
