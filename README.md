# YYZ AVOP DA Practice

A static web app. Open `index.html` in a browser; no installation is required. The app does not bundle the GTAA map or manual figures. Map questions link to GTAA’s official April 2024 site map in a separate tab, and the three diagram questions use original simplified illustrations.

## Format

- Mock Exam: 26 multiple-choice airside-rule questions, three diagram questions, and 20 map-location checks under Question 30.
- Quick Practice: 12 rule questions, one randomly selected diagram question, and all 20 map-location checks.
- Map Test Only: a standalone 20-question map session with its own score and retry option. It emphasizes 13 labelled apron/taxiway entrance markers, including AD, AE, AF, AG, AT, AJ, AK, AL, AM, DQ, DR, DT, and DV; it also includes terminal/apron features and the North/South Service Roads. Other roads and highways are excluded. Open the linked official map in another tab while answering.
- Questions, answer options, and map locations are randomized each session.
- Passing requires all map locations correct and no more than three errors on the other questions.
- Progress and scores are not saved.

## Difficulty and source basis

The linked Fellow Drivers quiz uses concise recall and application questions, four or more answer choices, definitions, and diagram-based right-of-way items. This app follows a comparable short-question format and mixes rule recall with applied scenarios while preserving the 30-question structure requested for this practice exam. Questions were independently rewritten from the supplied practice notes and checked against the supplied Airport Traffic Directives – AVOP DA (2026). The three diagram items use simplified, original illustrations of apron passenger paths and vehicle-corridor right-of-way.

This is an unofficial personal study aid, not an exam replica or a GTAA-approved product. The map-location tolerance is approximate for practice. The April 2024 site map may not reflect later changes. Follow the latest official directives and airside signs.
